Terrestrial memories

I used to have a telescope as a child, which I would sometimes take outside on a clear day to look at some stars.

I had no idea how to focus the telescope, so when I'd look through and see the plastic frame in front, I'd always think it was a satellite lol

WELCOME TO HELL

Thing is, I know they are supportive and wont judge me. The little devil in my brain has prevented me from doing so many things :(
