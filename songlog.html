Life We Live

Project Pat

I never really listened to music up until I was around 17, when I was introduced to Tyler the Creator. And ooooh man, I wanna say he changed my life and my view on music as a whole. I remember the summers of 2023 and 2024, when I would go out at midnight on my electric scooter and a skateboard on the bottom that I would precariously hold down with my foot to stop it from flying off as I cut corners up on my scooter hehe

One time I was on my scooter, I heard a car coming from behind so I moved over and as it drove past I felt something smash against my back. IT WAS AN EGG. The motherfuckers really just driveby egged someone who's on his scooter at 3am trying to listen to Tyler the Creator smh. Here's a picture if anyone cares

My favourite album is Wolf. It's all I would listen to at the time, and having just gone through a breakup it really made the songs more impactful. I don't listen to him as much these days, but I can't deny that he had a massive impact on my teen years and I'm really happy about it.
